  2. Wiring

  The robot uses a symmetrical structure, so the wiring rules are identical on the left and right
  sides. The instructions below cover only the right leg and left arm; wire the right arm and left leg
  by following the same method. The waist is a separate routing area and must be wired
  according to its own instructions.

   -​   CAN1 channel — motor 0x0d (trunk yaw) to power board: use harness RBW_Origin_13
        Body04, spec XT30(+2)-F.G.B-XT30U-F, length 50 cm.
   -​   CAN2 channel — motor 0x0e (left shoulder) to power board: use harness RBW_Origin_11
        Body02, spec XT30(+2)-F.G.B-XT30(+2)-F.G.B, length 20 cm.
   -​   CAN3 channel — motor 0x13 (right shoulder) to power board: use harness
        RBW_Origin_11 Body02, spec XT30(+2)-F.G.B-XT30(+2)-F.G.B, length 20 cm.

Motors connect to the power board following the principle left before right, legs before arms.
Motor 0x01 goes to CAN0, motor 0x0d to CAN1, motor 0x0e to CAN2, and motor 0x13 to CAN3.
The PCB is silkscreened accordingly — install according to the silkscreen.

2.6 Board-to-Board Connections

The RDKx5 uses the usb to typec harness, connecting via USB to the USB-to-CAN port on the
3-in-1 power board.

    1 Product Overview
   1.1 Product Introduction
        RoboParty Roboto Origin (hereinafter referred to as Roboto Origin) is a humanoid
   robot platform designed for research, education, and certain exhibition scenarios.
   Its most notable features are full open-source availability and a modular design. The
   entire hardware system follows a highly modular structural philosophy, making joint
   assembly simple and keeping maintenance and upgrade costs extremely low. This
   makes it ideal for frequent teaching experiments and engineering validation. The robot
   stands 1.25 meters tall with a compact structure, facilitating debugging and operation
   within laboratory environments. While maintaining functional integrity, Roboto Origin
   continuously undergoes topological optimization of mass and weight to enhance
   overall performance. The main structural components are machined using 3‑axis CNC,
   while the exterior parts are rapidly prototyped via 3D printing, significantly improving
   assembly efficiency and manufacturing accessibility. The outer material is made of
   rubber, which not only enhances the friendly feel during human‑robot interaction but
   also improves impact resistance and operational safety. As a fully open‑source robot
   platform, Roboto Origin aims to lower the barrier to R&D and promote the widespread
   application of robotics in teaching, research, and creative practice.

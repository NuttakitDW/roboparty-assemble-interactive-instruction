2.1 Pre-installation Requirements
     Motor Installation Precautions:                      Damiao Motor Debugging Documentation
     1.Before starting assembly, assign IDs to all motors and perform encoder
calibration according to the motor ID layout shown in the figure below.
     2.Do not strike the motor with excessive force to prevent damage to internal
components.
     3.If the rotor becomes stuck after tightening, first check whether the set screw
has not been fully backed out. If the issue is due to manufacturing tolerances, replace
the screw at the corresponding position with a screw of the same specification that
is 2 mm shorter.
     For detailed calibration procedures, please refer to the accompanying document
Damiao Motor Debugging Documentation.

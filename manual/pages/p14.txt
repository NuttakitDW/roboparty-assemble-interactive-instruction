  3.2 Joystick Dead Zone Elimination
       Instruction Clearance: Before pressing "B" for inference, be sure to manually
  move the left and right joysticks through their full range of motion and press the "LT"
  and "RT" buttons several times. This operation is used to eliminate the dead zone or
  drift of the hardware joysticks, preventing residual instructions from causing the
  robot to unexpectedly drift or sprint at the moment inference starts.
  3.3 Node Status Monitoring and Inference Initiation
        Background Status Confirmation: The operator must use the screen -r inference_
  node command on the host computer terminal to check the running status of the
  inference node. Only after confirming that there are no Error reports can the next step
  be taken.
        Start inference: Press the "B" button on the remote control to officially enter the
  model inference state. At this time, the supervisor needs to closely monitor the robot's
  gait, hold the hanger control rope, or be ready to press the "X" button at any time.
  4. Anomaly troubleshooting and emergency response
       During the full lifecycle of operation, in case of emergencies, please handle them
  according to the following logic:
       One-key Disable (Emergency Stop): If any abnormal behavior (such as high-
  frequency oscillation, joint overspeed, instability and toppling) occurs during the
  robot's reasoning process, please immediately press the "X" key to cut off the motor
  enable.
       Abnormal disconnection troubleshooting: If the robot experiences a non-human-
  triggered "sudden incapacitation" (collapsing to the ground) during inference, please
  maintain the scene as it is and immediately use screen -r inference_node to enter the
  terminal and check the error message. Common causes include:The algorithm layer
  has triggered the set safety limits (such as joint overrun, speed overrun). The hardware
  layer has triggered overload, overheat, or under-voltage protection.
       Log Retention: Before disconnecting the main power supply, it is recommended
  to export the Error log from the terminal first, so that the R&D team can locate the root
  cause of the problem.

Core Warnings
      High Dynamic Risk: The robot has an extremely high risk of instability and
toppling during gait debugging. Any suspension zeroing or gait inference test, must
be conducted in a controlled area equipped with a load-bearing safety hanger (fall
protection).
      Physical Emergency Stop Takes Precedence: In case of any uncontrollable
joint jitter, gait deviation, or structural interference, please immediately press the "X"
button on the remote control handle to trigger software disablement; if the software
malfunctions, please immediately press the physical emergency stop switchw to cut
off the main power supply.
1. Pre-power-on physical environment and hardware pre-check
(must be done before power-on)
    Before powering the main control and drive system, the operator must complete
the following verifications:

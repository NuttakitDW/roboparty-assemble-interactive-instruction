         Assembly Precautions

     1.Workbench Management
    1.1 Zoned Placement:
        Left side: Area for materials to be assembled (only materials required for the
    current process).
        Middle: Work area (lay anti-static mat, keep clean).
        Right side: Finished product / semi-finished product storage area.
    1.2 Foreign Object Prevention:
          It is strictly forbidden to place items unrelated to production on the workbench
    (e.g., mobile phones, water cups, keys) to prevent metal foreign objects from falling
    into the product and causing short circuits.

     2. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Standards
        Anti-static gloves: When touching all electronic components, circuit boards, and
    camera modules.
        Nitrile gloves: When using chemicals such as lubricating grease, thermal grease,
    threadlocker, etc.
        Hair/attire enforcement: Long hair must be tied up and tucked into a work cap;
    dangling jewelry (necklaces, bracelets) is strictly prohibited; cuffs must be tightened.
     3. Core Safety Operating Procedures
    3.1 Lithium Battery Safety Operation
         This product uses a high-energy-density lithium battery pack. Improper
    operation can easily cause a fire.
    Drop Disposal:
          If any battery pack is dropped (height > 30 cm) or subjected to a severe impact,
    it is strictly prohibited to install or use it again. Immediately affix a red label reading
    "Awaiting Scrap/Observation" and move it to a designated explosion-proof safety
    cabinet or sandbox for isolated observation for 24 hours.

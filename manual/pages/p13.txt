2.3 Specification for Floating Reset Operation
     Precondition for zero calibration: Before performing the Reset action, it must be
confirmed that all motors have completed absolute zero calibration. Do not perform
the reset operation before the motors are zero calibrated! It is recommended to
perform the reset operation using the hanger in the air.
     Pose Matching: When pressing the "A" button on the controller to perform Reset
(return the motor to the preset zero position), the operator needs to manually intervene
with the robot's limbs, ensuring that the robot's current physical pose is as close as
possible to the set Reset zero position pose.
     Position mutation warning: If the physical attitude deviates too much from the
zero position, a large step response (motion mutation) will occur instantaneously
during Reset, which can easily damage the motor reducer and structural components.

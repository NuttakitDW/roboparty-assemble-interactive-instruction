2.4 Battery Wiring

Screws and standoffs required:

   -​   Screws: m316 ×2, m412 ×2, m5*12 ×2
   -​   Standoffs: m317 ×4, m38+3 ×8

2.4.1 Battery Installation

   -​   Battery protection: Apply anti-vibration foam padding to the bottom and all four sides of
        the battery.
            -​ Purpose: to damp high-frequency vibration during motion and prevent the
                battery's outer sheath from being abraded, which would create a short-circuit
                risk.

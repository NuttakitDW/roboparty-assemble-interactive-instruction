                                          Assembly Precautions

Short Circuit Prevention:
     Before connecting the battery plug to the main board, the original factory
protective cover or insulating tape must remain in place. Exposing the positive and
negative terminals is strictly prohibited.
     Do not use metal tweezers or screwdrivers to touch the battery connector while
the system is powered on.
3.2 Mechanical Pinch Prevention and Operation
    Power-off operation: Installation, adjustment, and replacement of joints must be
performed with the emergency stop pressed and the power disconnected.
     Manual rotation: Do not insert fingers into the gaps of moving joints or into limit
slots, to prevent finger crushing or fracture caused by gravity-induced joint movement
or inertia closure.
     Screw fixing: When tightening flat head screws, avoid using excessive force to
prevent stripping the threads.
3.3 Chemical Use Safety
     Threadlocker: Apply only to the last 3–4 threads at the end of the screw. Do not
allow excess to spill and contaminate plastic parts (some plastics may become brittle
or crack when in contact with threadlocker).
     Waste disposal: Cleaning wipes or cotton swabs contaminated with chemicals
are hazardous waste and must be disposed of in designated yellow hazardous waste
bins. Mixing with ordinary waste is strictly prohibited.
3.4 Emergency Response Procedure
     When an abnormal situation occurs (smoke, fire, unusual noise, personal injury),
strictly follow the "Stop – Isolate – Report" procedure.
     Battery/Circuit Fire Response
     Step 1: Immediately press the main power emergency stop button.
     Step 2: Quickly evacuate to a safe distance (>3 meters) and loudly warn others.
     Step 3: Small flames: Use a dry powder fire extinguisher. Do NOT use water.
     		       Large fire: Immediately call the fire emergency number and evacuate
		            the workshop.

III. Installation Location Instructions

The insertion positions of the locating pins for the arm and leg are as shown in the
figure (taking the right hand and right leg as an example). It should be noted that the
illustrated scheme imposes a certain degree of over-constraint on the rotational
degrees of freedom of the leg. From a theoretical analysis perspective, only one locating
pin needs to be inserted into the leg joint to achieve posture positioning. However, in
actual engineering implementation, a multi-point constraint design scheme is adopted
to improve the stability and reliability of the calibration process.

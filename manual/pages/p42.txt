   2.3 Cylindrical Pin Calibration Plan for RoboParty Roboto Origin

   I. Purpose of Calibration Using Cylindrical Pins

   We realized that the calibration methods for 3D printing in the past had many
   deficiencies, such as factors like the accuracy and stiffness of 3D printed parts, as
   well as the time required to print calibration parts. Therefore, we proposed using
   cylindrical pins (GB119) to constrain the rotational degrees of freedom of each joint.
   The standard part calibration scheme aims to provide users with an alternative
   mechanical calibration method with high repeatability to achieve attitude constraint
   and zero alignment of each joint of the robot during the calibration process.

   II. Potential issues in cylindrical pin calibration

   It should be noted that this type of calibration method has relatively high requirements for
   the manufacturing tolerances of components, including but not limited to dimensional
   errors, positional errors, and shape errors. Due to the process differences between
   different machining suppliers and different machining batches, even when the nominal
   dimensions are consistent, the actual fitting effects of parts may vary. Therefore,
   during actual use, it may occur that the cylindrical locating pin cannot be smoothly
   inserted, or although it can be inserted, it cannot be fully inserted to the designed depth,
   thus affecting its effect on restricting the rotational degrees of freedom of the joint.
   If the locating pin cannot be inserted manually, you may choose to use a rubber hammer
   or other tools to insert it.
